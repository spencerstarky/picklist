✅ Tactile Pull Flow & Smart Routing

The Tactile Flow of Checking Items Off as You Pull

Enjoy instant physical confirmation with every item you grab from your racks or bins. Tapping an item delivers crisp tactile feedback, crosses it off in real time, and updates your remaining total along an optimized walking route:

  • 🔢
    Numeric Racks First

    Pure numerical items are sorted in ascending or descending sequence (e.g. #10024 → #10087 → #10115) for high-speed rack walking.

  • 📦
    Location Bins & Shelves Second

    Alpha-numeric bins and designated shelf coordinates (e.g. Bin B-104 → Shelf 3) are sequenced next to eliminate backtracking.

  • 🏷️
    Un-SKU'd Items Grouped Last

    Items without a location are placed cleanly at the end so your primary picking run never gets stalled.
