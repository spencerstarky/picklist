Crucial for iPhone & iPad: Enable iCloud Reminders

To see your daily pull lists on your mobile devices when walking the floor, Reminders must be synced to iCloud on every device you use:

💻 On your Mac:
  1. Open System Settings.
  2. Click Apple Account (sidebar) → iCloud.
  3. Under Saved to iCloud, click See All and switch Reminders to ON.
📱 On your iPhone / iPad:
  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap your Name at top → iCloud.
  3. Under Saved to iCloud, ensure Reminders is toggled ON.
💡 Pro Tip: Both devices must be signed in with the same Apple Account so pull list items appear on your phone instantly the moment you sync in Chrome!
