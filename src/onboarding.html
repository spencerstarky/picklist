Universal Google Tasks (Android, Windows, iOS & Web)

Zero Setup

Syncs your orders directly into Google Tasks under "Daily Pull List". Zero terminal commands, zero background daemons. Works seamlessly across Windows PCs, Android phones, iPhones, and Chromebooks.

  1. 1
    Connect Your Google Account:
    Click Sign in with Google above to grant one-click access to manage your pull lists.
  2. 2
    Get the Free Google Tasks Mobile App:
    Install the official Google Tasks app from the Google Play Store (Android) or App Store (iPhone). You can also view it right in the Gmail / Google Calendar desktop sidebar!
  3. 3
    Click Sync & Walk the Floor:
    Whenever orders sell, click Sync in Chrome. Your phone buzzes with your physical pull list sequenced in exact walking order.
